The Nine Checks — Does This Deserve to Be Built?

VC Pulse · VC fundraising pipeline intelligence for Vikram (B2B logistics SaaS, Seed) · MESA C4 Term 1 AI final · Nawaz Alam

⚡ Kill Switches

Any No means: don't build it.
01Problem real?YES

Vikram tracks 50 VCs by hand and can't tell who is warm now. Checking 50 people × 5 sources × twice a week ≈ 500 lookups a week, so outreach falls back to gut feel and mistimed emails.

02Workflow repeated?YES

Same loop every Monday and Thursday for the whole raise (3-6 months, ~50 runs): look up each investor, judge relevance, update the table.

03Input available?YES

Live public data exists through official feeds: Google News RSS (person; firm + logistics deals), VC blog/Substack RSS, Apple Podcasts API. Vikram's table is in Postgres. No LinkedIn/X scraping (terms).

📊 Sizing

Any No means: build something smaller.
04Output valuable?YES

Ranked board with a reason per VC: what each signal MEANS (thesis / deploying / complement / visible / overlap), Gemini's honest "why it matters for Vikram's startup" with caveats, clickable evidence, and an email draft that opens with that signal.

05Impact measurable?YES

Research time per session (~2-3 h → ~3 min review); % of outreach with a real hook; reply rate of signal-timed emails. AI quality measured: 100% precision, 89% recall on 57 hand-labelled items; every run logs its items, signals and cost.

08ROI worth it?YES

Measured AI cost ≈ $0.10 (~₹9) per run (Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite via the course OpenRouter key; cheapest model that scored best), ~₹75 a month at twice a week, vs 4-5 founder-hours saved every week of the raise.

🚧 Boundary

Any No means: put a human exactly here.
06Failure risk OK?YES → human at send

Ranking errors are cheap and caught by code checks + visible evidence → automated. A wrong email to an investor is costly → NOT automated.

👤 Human placed exactly here: Vikram reads the evidence and sends every email himself (draft only).
07Judgment protected?YES → human decides

Whom to pitch, what to say and how the relationship is going are founder judgment calls. The tool even refuses to call an off-topic deal a cold-email reason.

👤 Human placed exactly here: AI only recommends; Vikram decides, edits and sends; only he can change status, contact history and notes.
09Owner clear?YES

Each column has one owner, enforced by Postgres logins: pulse_engine = 4 signal columns; pulse_founder = Vikram's columns; owner login not on the server.

👤 Human placed exactly here: Vikram owns relationships and his columns; the builder owns sources, prompt and rules (public repo).
Verdict: BUILD IT. All three kill switches are YES and all sizing checks are YES, so we build the full system rather than a smaller one. The boundary checks tell us exactly where a human stays: Vikram sends every email and owns his notes. The automation stops at "recommend + draft".
The Cut (what we deliberately did NOT automate): sending emails · writing his notes or contact history (blocked by the database) · scraping LinkedIn/X · letting the AI set the score (AI classifies and explains; code calculates).
